construction AI Solutions

checkroom

Virtual try-on overlay

AR/ML model renders wishlisted garments on user's body type from a single selfie or saved measurements.

Smart size recommendation

Uses body measurements + purchase history + brand-specific size charts to predict fit. Shows "Likely fits you" badge on wishlist card.

photo_library

Customer photo aggregation

ML clusters real-customer photos by body type so users see how the product looks on someone similar to them.

video_library

Auto-surface review videos

NLP identifies review videos that mention fit/size and pins them to the wishlist item. No user effort needed.

Conversion hypothesis: Users sitting at 3/5 purchase intent move to 4/5 when they can confirm fit visually — eliminating the primary reason to delay.

Open-text: "If you could improve one thing about the wishlist experience"

11 responses (AI summary from Google Forms)

📦 Inventory management

Users want out-of-stock items separated into a distinct section so the actionable wishlist is not cluttered. Multiple respondents mentioned frustration at having to scroll past unavailable items.

💰 Pricing & promotions

Feedback included requests for a pricing dropdown notification and a clear preference for highlighting discounted products directly on wishlist items. Users want to be told when their wishlisted item goes on sale — not have to check manually.

📐 Size & fit clarity

Several users mentioned wanting to see fit information (sizing comparison, "will this fit me?") directly from the wishlist without navigating to the product page. The friction of clicking through kills purchase momentum.

🔍 Better organisation

Requests for folders/collections within the wishlist to separate occasions (work, casual, wedding season). Currently it's a flat list that becomes overwhelming at 30+ items.
