Phase 1 — Quantitative discovery

1

Funnel + revisits + time-to-purchase

Never revisited / revisited not bought / purchased / removed. Zero revisits = forgetting; many revisits + no buy = deliberation. Converter latency: decisive vs long-tail.

2

Stock + price trajectory

Size/color OOS at intended purchase vs add. Price drop/rise/flat for converters vs non-converters (sale-wait test).

3

Wishlist size + category

Power-wishlisters (100+) vs light (5–10). Occasion-wear vs basics — different psychology, do not average.

4

Search-before-drop + cart baseline

Same-item reviews vs competing products before abandon. Wishlist vs cart abandonment — wishlist-specific or general hesitation.
