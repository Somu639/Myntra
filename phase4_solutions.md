# Phase 4 — Solution ideation (non-monetary constraint)

Once Phase 3 has the **real mix**, solutions map cleanly onto friction types. This phase is **illustrative directions**, not a build list. Do not prescribe a solution before the opportunity map is filled.

**Constraint:** no monetary incentive (no coupon, cashback, wallet credit, or “₹X off your wishlist”). Reassurance, information, comparison, timing, and list architecture only.

**North-star:** still wishlist / consideration → purchase conversion on Myntra — but only among cohorts where conversion is the right metric (see bookmarking).

Public-VOC sketches in `opportunity_proposal.md` are companion ideas. They do not skip Phase 3 ranking or this constraint.

---

## Output

A **solution sketch per ranked friction** — 2–4 non-monetary directions each — tagged to the Phase 3 cell (category × tenure × wishlist-size) where that friction concentrates. Plus one architectural bet: split inspiration vs ready-to-buy.

---

## Illustrative directions (not prescriptions)

### Fit / size doubt

- Fit-prediction from the user’s purchase and return history
- Richer size-specific reviews (“runs small”) and body-type filters
- AR / virtual try-on
- Size-exchange **guarantee messaging** — non-monetary reassurance, not a discount

### Comparison paralysis

- In-wishlist comparison view (side-by-side across saved similar items)
- “Most saved by people who also saved this”
- Editorial styling picks that collapse near-duplicates

### Trust / validation gap

- Shareable wishlist for friend feedback
- Review summarization focused on fit / quality (not just star ratings)
- UGC / try-on video surfacing on the PDP and wishlist item

### Occasion timing

- Let users tag wishlist items with an event / date
- Remind as the date approaches — **timing-based, not price-based**

### Forgetting / decay

- Smart resurfacing of **high-intent** items (revisit frequency), not every saved SKU equally
- Wishlist decluttering nudges for aged, never-reopened items

### Bookmarking-only users (cohort, not a PDP widget)

Do **not** force conversion. Separate an **inspiration board** from a **ready to buy** list so the headline metric targets genuine-intent users, not moodboarders.

This itself may be the **single biggest lever**: mixing both cohorts dilutes wishlist→purchase. If Phase 3 shows heavy lists converting like bookmarks, fix the metric and the IA before shipping fit-AR to everyone.

### Price-wait (if it ranks)

The constraint forbids markdown. If Phase 3 sizes this large, the allowed moves are information and timing (price trajectory since save, restock waitlist) — **not** a coupon. If the only viable lever is a discount, say so and leave it out of this backlog.

### Style regret

Usually a remove / declutter outcome, not a conversion play. Do not “win back” items the user has decided against.

## How this sits next to Phase 3 and Phase 5

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Ideate only on **top-ranked** Phase 3 rows | Pretty solutions for rare frictions waste the experiment budget |
| Bind each sketch to a **cell** (e.g. footwear × new user) | Platform-wide widgets hide where the friction lives |
| No discounts in the sketch | This program’s constraint; price-wait may simply not be solvable here |
| Phase 5 picks 1–2 bets | Ideation is a menu; validation is a test |

Do not ship a comparison view because it sounds right. Wait for comparison paralysis to rank. Do not A/B “wishlist coupons.” That is out of scope.
